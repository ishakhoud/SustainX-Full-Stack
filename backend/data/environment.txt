Environmental Protection

Environmental protection involves practices that preserve natural resources,
reduce pollution, and protect ecosystems.

Waste Management

Waste management involves collecting, sorting, recycling, and disposing of
waste responsibly. Waste should be separated into biodegradable,
recyclable, and hazardous categories where appropriate.

Plastic Pollution

Plastic pollution occurs when plastic waste accumulates in the environment.
Single-use plastics contribute to pollution in oceans, rivers, and soil.
Reusable bags, bottles, and containers can help reduce plastic waste.

Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperatures and weather
patterns. Human activities, especially burning fossil fuels, contribute
significantly to current global warming.

Recycling

Recycling converts waste materials into reusable materials. Paper, glass,
metals, and certain plastics can be recycled, depending on local facilities
and recycling guidelines.

Water Conservation

Water conservation involves reducing unnecessary water consumption.
Fixing leaks, using water-efficient fixtures, and avoiding unnecessary
water wastage can help conserve freshwater resources.
